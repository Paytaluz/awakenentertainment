FAQ

Answers up front.

What does a branding project include?+
Typically a logo and brand system, colour and type guidelines, and key visuals for launch. We scope every project to what your brand actually needs.
Can you handle our monthly social media design?+
Yes — we plan monthly content calendars and produce daily creative for brands like MPT and Gery, so your feed stays consistent and on-brand.
Do you work with international brands in Myanmar?+
Yes — Gery (Garudafood), MacArthur's Scotch Whisky, Kirin Ichiban and Becozinc (Dr. Reddy's) trusted us to bring their brands to Myanmar audiences.
Can you also shoot photos and video?+
Yes. With Awaken Production in the same family we create locally shot photography and video — like the stock imagery we produced for Becozinc.
Do you design websites and apps too?+
We design the UI/UX in-house and build it with Awaken Tech — one team from first sketch to launch.

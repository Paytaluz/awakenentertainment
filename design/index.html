Our process

Four steps from
brief to brand.

Every Awaken Design project follows the same path, so you always know what happens next — and nothing ships until it’s right.

01

Discover & Research

Before a single pixel, we immerse ourselves in your brand story, audience and competitive landscape. Every creative decision starts with deep understanding.

  • Brand story & audience research
  • Competitive landscape review
  • A clear creative brief
02

Concept & Direction

We present multiple visual directions — mood boards, type pairings, colour stories — then refine the one that resonates. Nothing moves to production without alignment.

  • Mood boards & type pairings
  • Colour stories & visual directions
  • Sign-off before production
03

Design & QA

Every asset, every format, every screen size. Our design-system approach means nothing ships until it passes internal review — pixel-perfect and production-ready.

  • Every asset, format and size
  • Design-system approach
  • Internal review before anything ships
04

Deliver & Support

Our team stays available after delivery, so your identity grows as your business does.

  • Fast turnaround on revisions
  • Ongoing brand guidance
  • Asset extensions as you grow

What does Awaken Design do?+

Brand identity, advertising and campaign key visuals, social media and campaign design, UI/UX, art direction and photography — for brands in Myanmar and international partners.

Which brands have you worked with?+

MPT, Gery, MacArthur’s, Becozinc, Wave Money, Kirin Ichiban, Grand Royal, AWBA, MicroMoney, Handicap International, Myanmar Link, UMG and Ananda.

How does a project start?+

With a conversation. We learn your brand story, audience and landscape first, then present visual directions for you to choose from. Call, WhatsApp or email us to set it up.

Can we book your studio?+

Yes. The Mayangone studio is open for bookings Mon–Sat, 9 AM – 6 PM, for shoots, workshops and presentations. Lighting and camera kit from Awaken Production is available on request. Book on WhatsApp at +95 9255762417.

Which tools do you design in?+

Figma, Illustrator, Photoshop, InDesign, After Effects and Adobe XD.

Do you support us after delivery?+

Yes. We stay available for fast revisions, brand guidance and new assets as your brand grows.

Questions & Answers

Why is zinc important for human health?

Zinc is a nutrient the body needs to stay healthy. It's found in cells throughout the body and helps the immune system defend against bacteria and viruses. The body also uses zinc to make proteins and DNA.

What are the benefits of Zinc?

Zinc supports a healthy immune system, healthy growth during childhood, and the body's natural wound-healing process.

Is it better to take Becozinc in the morning or at night?

Becozinc can be taken in the morning or at night — many people find mornings convenient for daily energy. Follow the dosage on the pack, or your doctor's advice.

Does Becozinc cause weight gain?

Overall diet has far more impact on weight than a daily supplement. Becozinc is a nutritional supplement, not a weight-gain product.

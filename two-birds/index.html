Who We Are

A Family of Brands, Built Since 1997

For more than 25 years, Two Birds Manufacturing has been producing the candies and snacks found in corner shops, school bags, and family homes across Myanmar.

What began as a single candy line has grown into a portfolio of four trusted brands — Ximi, Kido, Ki Ki, and Lucky — each made with the same commitment to quality and consistency that's kept us in business for over two decades.

Today, our products are manufactured across two facilities — Yangon and Mandalay — and distributed to shops and households nationwide.

Who We Are

A Snack Myanmar Has Always Loved

Ma-Yway — the roasted rice cracker snack — has been part of Myanmar snacking culture for generations. It's the treat shared at school, at work, around the family table, and with grandparents telling stories.

Lucky brings that beloved snack into every generation's hands with a golden crunch, honest ingredients, and a promise: every pack opens a little burst of joy.

Every venue has media hiding in it — walls, gates, tents, floors, staff, even the hours after dark. We walk the site, name every format, and hand brands a menu instead of a blank hoarding.

How a venue becomes a catalogue

The same four-step process behind every place we map.

01

Walk the site

Photograph every wall, gate, tent, and sightline — day and night.

02

Name the formats

Group what we found into repeatable placements: print, build, or light.

03

Map & tag

Pin every format to the plan and tag it by zone, size, and production method.

04

Price & produce

Hand the brand a menu — ready to select, budget, and build.

Our mission is to put patient capital to productive use — backing businesses with identifiable demand, credible operators, and a measurable path to profitability across Myanmar's growing economy.

We seek long-term capital partners who want an ownership interest in business growth without assuming responsibility for daily operations. Investment selection, financial oversight, and portfolio reporting sit within a common framework, while operating teams remain responsible for delivery within each business.

Awaken is targeting US$1 million to US$5 million in investment capital, deployed in stages according to opportunity quality, verified funding needs, and agreed investment terms.

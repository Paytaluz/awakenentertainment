FAQ

Answers up front.

How long does a typical digital campaign take to set up?+
Most campaigns are fully set up and live within 2–3 weeks. This includes strategy, creative production, audience setup, and technical tracking. Larger projects with web builds may take 4–8 weeks.
Do you work with businesses outside Myanmar?+
Yes — while our roots are in the Myanmar market, we work with regional and international clients across Southeast Asia and beyond.
What platforms do you run ads on?+
We primarily work with Meta (Facebook & Instagram), Google Ads, TikTok Ads, and LinkedIn. We recommend the right mix based on your audience and goals.
How do you measure success?+
We align on KPIs at the start of every engagement — reach, leads, conversions, or ROAS. You get clear weekly and monthly reporting with no vanity metrics.
Can you handle both digital marketing and web development?+
Absolutely. As part of the Awaken family we bring together Digital, Design, Tech, Production, and PR under one roof — no agency handoff, no miscommunication.

01 — Understanding Ximi Jelly

Two ideas, kept deliberately apart

Ximi Jelly doesn't need one big idea trying to do two jobs at once. This proposal splits the work in half — and lets each half do only one job, well.

The Campaign

XimiSnap

Turns everyday bus stops into Yangon's biggest selfie trail — not train stations, bus stops, parked exactly where more than a million commuters already are, every day.

The Cause

The Ximi Giving Wall

A pure CSR activation built on a simple act of generosity — a landmark people pass every day, with no hashtag required to take part.

The Bridge

Where they touch

Every approved selfie from XimiSnap adds real money to the Wall's giving pool — the only place the two ideas touch. They stay creatively separate everywhere else.

02 — The Campaign & The Cause

Two separate ideas, on purpose

One built for reach, one built for trust.

The Campaign — Objectives

  • Own the everyday commute
    Turn Yangon's bus stops into Ximi's own content trail, not just a backdrop.
  • Drive real volume
    Selfies, reach, and shareability — measured in posts and impressions.
  • Feel like everyday Yangon
    Native to how people already move through the city, township by township.

The CSR — Objectives

  • Be seen giving
    A physical, public act of generosity — not a quiet donation.
  • Maximize footfall reach
    Placed where Yangon already gathers, for months, not one day.
  • Earn trust, not attention
    No hashtag required to participate — generosity needs no strings.

Risk & Contingency

What could go wrong, and the fallback

Early-October rain

Risk

October is a monsoon transition month in Yangon (avg. ~176mm) before Nov–Dec turn reliably dry.

Fallback

Weatherproof panels from day one; hero film + KOL shoots buffered to late October if needed.

Low early participation

Risk

A new mechanic can be slow to catch on in its first two weeks.

Fallback

KOL Wave 1 front-loaded at launch; seed content shows exactly how to take part.

Site permit delays

Risk

Local approvals for physical installations can slip.

Fallback

Backup locations pre-identified in every Wave 1 township before launch.

Prop loss or damage

Risk

Handheld photo props are the most exposed physical item.

Fallback

Props are deliberately low-cost and replaceable — the panel itself is the only durable, higher-value asset.

Source: Weather Atlas & Sunheron climate data, Yangon monthly averages.

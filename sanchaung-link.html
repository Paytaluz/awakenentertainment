Part one · the walls

Seven ways to use one hoarding run

Every wall format is made one of three ways — that is how we brief vendors and budget.

A

Printed on the wall

Vinyl print or hand-paint with weatherproof laminate. Fastest to produce, easiest to refresh.

01 Mega-mural · 02 Sequential panels · 03 Photo wall · 06 Flavor strip
B

Built off the wall

Fabricated foamboard, ACM or timber pieces fixed to the hoarding structure.

04 3D relief props · 05 Corner wrap
C

Lit onto the wall

Zero print. One projector and a power point turn the same wall into night media.

07 Projection mapping
01

Mega-mural / billboard wrap

One artwork across the full hoarding run — the venue's biggest single canvas, seen the moment people arrive.

Print30–60 m runArrival sightline
02

Sequential panel storytelling

Chapters read while walking — pour, clink, laugh. Repetition builds recall by the time guests reach the stalls.

Print3–8 panelsWalkway walls
03

Instagrammable photo wall

A backdrop people step into — wings and props sized for a phone frame. Every photo posts the brand for free.

A dimensional bottle breaking above the wall line — visible over the fence from the street outside, not just inside.

Corner wrap

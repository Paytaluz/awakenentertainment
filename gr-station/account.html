How a claim works

  1. Your points reach a prize step.
  2. Tap Claim on this page.
  3. A one-time voucher with a QR code appears.
  4. Show it at the vendor before it expires.
  5. The vendor scans or types the code in the portal.
  6. The prize is handed over and the cloud marks it claimed.

The prototype

Four views on one ledger: the public board, the player account, the vendor portal and the brand dashboard. Everything you change on one page shows up on the others.

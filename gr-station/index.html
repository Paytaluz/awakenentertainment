Register and start playing

Four steps, one mobile number

Step 1

Scan the QR

Point your camera at the Station. The QR is on the logo plate and on the screen.

Step 2

Your number

A local mobile number is the only ID needed. Numbers are stored encrypted.

Step 3

Enter the code

An SMS code and an 18+ tick confirm it. One phone number is one account.

Step 4

You are in

The Station assigns the account and the round starts.

More points, bigger prize

The points ladder

Points come from all three events: the brand and general quiz, the series question, and the EPL quiz and prediction.

Claim each step once per season at the vendor. Prizes are non-alcohol, won by skill, and switched on only when counsel clears them. Steps and prizes are a proposal, tuned in the pilot.

Prototype notes

How this demo behaves

No server

The “cloud” is a ledger in your own browser. Nothing is sent anywhere, and nothing is shared between devices.

Demo sign-in

Any local mobile number works, and the SMS code is shown on screen. Vendor codes are listed on the portal page.

Start over

Reset the ledger to its seeded state at any time.

The prototype

Four views on one ledger: the public board, the player account, the vendor portal and the brand dashboard. Everything you change on one page shows up on the others.

Compliance envelope

Free to play, no purchase or stake. Skill-based ranking, never a random draw. 18+ only, ID checked at collection. Non-alcohol rewards. No betting language.

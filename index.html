Who We Are

One Holding.
Many Frontiers.

Awaken Entertainment is Myanmar's investment holding fund identifying and scaling high-potential businesses across entertainment, digital platforms, food & beverage, and consumer strategy.

We partner with founders who understand local culture — providing not just capital, but operational support, creative direction, and market access to turn bold ideas into lasting businesses.

From streaming platforms to lifestyle destinations, our portfolio reflects the breadth of Myanmar's emerging economy and the ambition of its next generation of entrepreneurs.

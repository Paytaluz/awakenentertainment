    1 / 4

    40.0 million people are online.

    That is 72.4% of Myanmar. The median age is 30.4 and there are 62.5M mobile connections — a young, mobile-first market.

    2 / 4

    TikTok reaches four in five adults.

    32.2M adults can be reached on TikTok, against 11.1M on Facebook. Instagram and LinkedIn are niche.

    3 / 4

    And the gap is widening fast.

    In one year TikTok’s adult reach rose from 21.0M to 32.2M (+53%), while Facebook’s fell from 13.7M to 11.1M (−19%).

    4 / 4

    Who is watching.

    25–34 is the largest adult age band, at 15.8% of people. TikTok’s adult ad audience is now 54.5% female — up from 38.8% a year earlier.

    Video is the new shelf.

    Most adults can now be reached on TikTok. Short, native stories decide which brands people remember when they buy.

    Earn it, then buy it.

    Content people choose to watch grows reach at low cost. Paid media then scales what already works — not the other way round.

    Loyalty lives in chats.

    Facebook Groups, Viber and Telegram are where customers ask, share and buy. We plan them in from day one.

        Answers up front
        
      
        
        
        How fast can we start?
        Most campaigns are set up and live within 2–3 weeks — strategy, content, audiences and tracking included. Bigger projects with films or web builds take longer.
        
        
        How do you measure success?
        We agree KPIs with you at the start — reach, engagement, leads, conversions or ROAS — then report weekly and review monthly. No vanity metrics.
        
        
        How long until we see real reach?
        A typical brand we run reaches its first million people within 3–6 months. That is our benchmark, not a promise: category, content and budget all play a part.
        
        
        Which platforms do you advertise on?
        Meta (Facebook and Instagram), TikTok, Google and LinkedIn. We recommend the mix that fits your audience and goals.
        
        
        Can we see your talent database?
        Yes. Ask for an MCDB viewer login in your brief and we will set one up, so we can shortlist talent together.
        
        
        Do you work with brands outside Myanmar?
        Yes. Our roots are in Myanmar, and we also work with regional and international brands that want to reach Myanmar audiences.

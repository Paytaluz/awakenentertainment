    Answers up front
    
      
    
        
    How fast can we start?
    Most campaigns are set up and live within 2–3 weeks — strategy, content, audiences and tracking included. Bigger projects with films or web builds take longer.
    
        
    How do you measure success?
    We agree KPIs with you at the start — reach, engagement, leads, conversions or ROAS — then report weekly and review monthly. No vanity metrics.
    
        
    How long until we see real reach?
    A typical brand we run reaches its first million people within 3–6 months. That is our benchmark, not a promise: category, content and budget all play a part.
    
        
    Which platforms do you advertise on?
    Meta (Facebook and Instagram), TikTok, Google and LinkedIn. We recommend the mix that fits your audience and goals.
    
        
    Can we see your talent database?
    Yes. Ask for an MCDB viewer login in your brief and we will set one up, so we can shortlist talent together.
    
        
    Do you work with brands outside Myanmar?
    Yes. Our roots are in Myanmar, and we also work with regional and international brands that want to reach Myanmar audiences.
